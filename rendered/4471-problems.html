2. booking-v3 form hint

Tenants on booking-v3 (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) are day-first, but the form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it their way. The parser fix will not touch this. Form channel only. Ticket: issue 2.

3. One-day moves

Five reports (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620) describe a date one day off, not a month flip. One client in Lisbon got Tuesday and one in Toronto Monday; one invite arrived before the booking existed. That points at time zones or clocks, not parsing. The desk notes already single them out, but filing by tenant puts them under the parser. Cause not yet confirmed. Ticket: issue 3.
